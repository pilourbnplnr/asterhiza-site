WNBA All-Star Weekend · Chase Center + Moscone Center · July 30–31, 2027

Welcome
to the Bay

A working guide for out-of-town fans headed to San Francisco for All-Star weekend — how to get here, where to sleep, and where to eat, from someone who's watched every Valkyries home game at Chase Center. Confirmed so far: the Shooting Stars/3-Point Contest and the Game itself at Chase Center, plus the sixth annual WNBA Live fan festival at Moscone Center.

REALITY CHECK — SF proper is expensive. You don't have to sleep there. The East Bay, the Peninsula, and the South Bay are all a short BART, Caltrain, or ferry ride from Chase Center — this guide is built around that fact.
Start here

Find your route

Chase Center sits in Mission Bay; Moscone Center (home of WNBA Live) sits in SoMa, about 2.5 miles north. Pick where you're coming from — every route below already exists for Valkyries game days or SF's convention calendar, so this isn't guesswork.

The value play

East Bay, city by city

The East Bay is where the real savings live — and it's not just Oakland. Every city below sits on BART, so "further from SF" doesn't mean "harder to reach." Ferry access is called out separately since it's Oakland/Alameda-only.

Budget

Oakland

Budget

The closest East Bay option and the only one with direct ferry service to Pier 48 — no transfers, no BART. Also has a real restaurant and bar scene of its own if you want a night away from the SF crush.

  • Ferry: direct to Chase Center, ~25–35 min
  • BART: multiple downtown Oakland stations, ~20–25 min to Powell St

Alameda

Budget

Quieter and more residential than Oakland, with the second direct ferry terminal (Main St). A good pick if you want the boat commute without downtown Oakland's energy.

  • Ferry: direct to Chase Center from Main St. terminal
  • Free parking at the terminal if you're driving in

Berkeley

Budget

A bit further out, but Downtown Berkeley BART is a straight shot down the line to Powell St with no transfers. Worth it if you want a college-town feel and typically softer rates than Oakland proper on a big event weekend.

  • BART: Downtown Berkeley → Powell St, ~30 min direct
  • No ferry access — BART only

San Leandro

Budget

South of Oakland on the BART line, generally the softest rates of this group since it's less of a destination in its own right. Straightforward direct BART ride, just a longer one.

  • BART: San Leandro → Powell St, ~35–40 min direct
  • No ferry access — BART only

Hayward

Budget

The furthest of this group, and likely the cheapest per night. Still a direct BART line into the city — just budget more time and mind the last-train cutoff after night games.

  • BART: Hayward → Powell St, ~40–45 min direct
  • No ferry access — BART only

Oakland

~20–35 min to SF · Ferry + BART · the closest East Bay base
Budget
Transit
Ferry → Chase Center

SF Bay Ferry from Jack London Square direct to Pier 48, a 10–15 min walk from the arena. This is Oakland's signature route — no transfers.

BART → Chase Center

12th St Oakland City Center or 19th St Oakland to Powell St (~20–25 min), then Muni T Third/S Shuttle to UCSF/Chase Center.

BART → Moscone Center

Same BART ride to Powell St or Montgomery St, then a 10–12 min walk down Market/2nd St, or one Muni Metro stop to Yerba Buena/Moscone.

Ferry → Moscone Center

Ferry to the Ferry Building (regular Oakland/Alameda route, not the Pier 48 special) puts you a 12–15 min walk from Moscone.

Further out, and Caltrain/BART trips get longer — but this is Silicon Valley proper, with its own restaurant scene, its own Pride history, and (for stadium events) its own convention-hotel supply. Included here: every South Bay city that sits directly on BART, Caltrain, or Amtrak/ACE.

San José

~60–80 min to SF · BART + Caltrain + Amtrak/ACE · Silicon Valley's own downtown
Budget
Transit

Money-saving tips

  • Your event ticket is your transit pass. On Valkyries game days, the ticket doubles as all-day Muni fare — no separate transit cost if this holds for All-Star.
  • Base out of the East Bay. Oakland/Alameda consistently runs cheaper than SF proper, and the ferry ride is part of the experience, not a downgrade.
  • Skip the arena food when you can. Mission Rock is a 10–15 min walk and noticeably cheaper than concessions, with better food.
  • Watch the last train. BART and Caltrain don't hold for late finishes — know your last departure before you leave the hotel.
  • Book ferry tickets in advance. Clipper isn't accepted on the special Chase Center service — it's a separate ticket through the app.
  • Don't assume you need a car. Every route in this guide gets you to Chase Center without driving or parking fees.
